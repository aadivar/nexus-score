## Licensing

Research Nexus Score is open. There is one narrow exception, for
commercial AI training.

**Source code** — licensed under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). Use, modify, and
distribute freely under the AGPL, including the network-use provisions.

**Scores and data** — licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-DATA). Free for any use, including commercial,
provided you attribute the source. This covers libraries, publishers,
researchers, journalists, discovery platforms, and almost everyone else.

**Commercial AI training and product integration** — reserved. If you
are an AI company or building a commercial AI product and want to use
Nexus Score data or methodology for training, fine-tuning, or as a
retrieval / grounding corpus, please get in touch first. See
[AI-USE.md](AI-USE.md) for details.

**Upstream Crossref metadata** — remains under Crossref's own terms (CC0).
