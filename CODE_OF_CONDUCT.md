# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Research Nexus Score is public infrastructure for the scholarly community. We are committed to providing a welcoming and constructive environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Engaging with respect and good faith
- Providing constructive feedback with actionable suggestions
- Accepting that the scoring methodology is a work in progress and open to challenge
- Crediting others' contributions and ideas
- Focusing on what is best for the scholarly community

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Trolling or deliberately unconstructive comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any community channels.

## Methodology Disagreements

This project invites challenges to its scoring methodology. Disagreeing with weights, dimensions, or design decisions is not only acceptable — it's how the methodology improves. File an issue, make your case, propose an alternative. The only requirement is that critique be specific and constructive.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to [aadi@nexus-score.org](mailto:aadi@nexus-score.org). All reports will be reviewed and responded to promptly.
